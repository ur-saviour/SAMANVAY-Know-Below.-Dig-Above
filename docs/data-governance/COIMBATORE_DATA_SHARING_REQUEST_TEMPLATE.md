# SAMANVAY — Coimbatore Pilot Data-Sharing Request Template

**Use:** Adapt this draft through a school, college, research mentor or other responsible institutional contact. Do not send personal identity documents or sensitive personal information in an initial enquiry. Do not request restricted utility routes through an ordinary personal email account.

## A. Request to Coimbatore City Municipal Corporation

**Suggested recipient:** Commissioner, Coimbatore City Municipal Corporation  
**Official public contact route:** commr.coimbatore@tn.gov.in  
**Official portal:** https://ccmc.gov.in/

**Subject:** Request for guidance and non-sensitive GIS / infrastructure datasets for the SAMANVAY academic pilot

Respected Sir/Madam,

We are developing SAMANVAY, an academic prototype focused on improving coordination between road-owning authorities and public-utility agencies before road excavation, repair and reconstruction. The proposed first study area is within Coimbatore City Municipal Corporation.

We request your guidance on the appropriate department or designated officer who may advise us on the availability and permitted academic use of the following non-sensitive records:

1. Current ward and zone boundaries, official ward codes and the latest delimitation reference.
2. Municipal road inventory, road classification and road-owning authority, preferably in a GIS-compatible format.
3. Road-cutting permission workflow, required documents, review stages, restoration conditions and status fields.
4. Planned and completed road works, resurfacing and restoration records, preferably as anonymised or non-sensitive sample records.
5. Stormwater-drain and underground-drainage asset-register metadata, including asset identifiers, type, responsible division, last update and available survey/as-built source.
6. Any approved, non-sensitive GIS sample or demonstration dataset that can be used for an academic prototype.

For each dataset, we would be grateful for its data dictionary, coordinate reference system, geometry type, source/update date, known accuracy limitations, licence/attribution terms and the correct permission process. We will not publish exact utility geometry or restricted operational information. If detailed records require a formal agreement or secure transfer process, please advise the appropriate procedure rather than sending them through an unapproved channel.

We would also appreciate the designation or contact route of a GIS / Engineering / Roads / Stormwater / UGD nodal officer who can clarify asset ownership and the correct department-level workflow.

SAMANVAY is a prototype and does not claim to provide statutory excavation clearance or to replace the Corporation's approval process. All proposed proximity alerts are advisory and require confirmation from the responsible authority.

Thank you for your time and guidance.

Yours faithfully,  
[Name of student / project team]  
[School / institution and supervising teacher or mentor]  
[Official institutional contact details]

## B. Request to TNeGA / TNGIS

**Suggested route:** TNGIS support and the Directorate of e-Governance  
**Portal:** https://tngis.tn.gov.in/  
**Support page:** https://tngis.tn.gov.in/apps.html  
**Directorate:** https://deg.tn.gov.in/

**Subject:** Academic access enquiry — TNGIS layer catalogue, metadata and API guidance for SAMANVAY

Respected Sir/Madam,

We are developing SAMANVAY, an academic prototype for multi-agency coordination of roads and underground public utilities. We are beginning with a Coimbatore pilot and would like to use TNGIS only in accordance with the portal's current terms and the data-owning departments' permissions.

Could you please guide us on:

1. The current catalogue of layers available for registered download and the process for academic access.
2. The current list of common administrative layers, including ward, town, taluk and local-body boundaries.
3. Available API, WMS/WFS or other supported integration documentation, including authentication and rate limits.
4. Dataset-level metadata: owner, publisher, CRS, geometry type, update date, scale/accuracy and licence.
5. The process for requesting department-specific layers or a restricted pilot dataset from the relevant owner.
6. The written approval required for use within a non-commercial academic prototype, including display, derived analytics, caching and redistribution restrictions.

We understand that portal availability does not automatically grant permission for commercial use, redistribution or access to restricted utility information. We will retain attribution and data lineage and follow the applicable terms.

## C. Request to TWAD Board

**Official portal:** https://www.twadboard.tn.gov.in/  
**e-ODB portal:** https://twadeodb.tn.gov.in/

Ask for the correct Coimbatore-region scheme owner and whether an approved academic sample can be provided for:
- Combined water-supply scheme boundaries and asset ownership.
- Water-treatment plants, pumping stations and reservoirs.
- Water transmission/distribution network metadata and as-built record availability.
- Sewerage scheme boundaries, treatment plants and pumping assets where TWAD is the owner.
- Maintenance division, update cadence, data dictionary, CRS, survey date and accuracy.
- Secure data-sharing and permission requirements for any detailed alignment.

Do not assume that all Coimbatore municipal water or sewer assets are TWAD-owned; request scheme-by-scheme ownership confirmation.

## D. Request to TNPDCL / electricity distribution authority

**Official portal:** https://www.tangedco.com/en/

Ask the current local distribution-circle office to identify its authorised GIS / asset-record nodal officer and clarify whether a sanitised academic sample can be shared for:
- Underground cable/duct asset metadata, where recorded.
- Feeder/substation/transformer reference metadata suitable for the approved use.
- Planned cable-laying or maintenance work records.
- Survey/as-built source, CRS, accuracy, update date and asset ownership.
- Access classification, secure transfer route and restrictions.

Do not request consumer personal information or publish exact critical-asset routes. Any detailed route access must be approved by the utility and restricted to explicitly authorised roles.

## E. Attachments to prepare through the institution

- A one-page SAMANVAY project summary and intended academic use.
- A simple system architecture diagram showing role-based access and the fact that exact routes are not public.
- A short data dictionary listing the fields requested.
- A letter from the supervising teacher / institution, if required by the authority.
- A statement that no dataset will be republished or used beyond the granted permission.
